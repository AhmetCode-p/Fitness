FORGE

Haftalik Antrenman Programi Olusturucu v5

Forge nedir?

Haftalik antrenman programlarini olustur, takip et ve tek bir kod ile paylas. Tamamen tarayicida calisir, hesap gerekmez.

Ozellikler

  • 7 gunluk program sihirbazi — her gune kas grubu ve hareket ekle.
  • Antrenman takibi — yapilan hareketleri isaretle, kilolari yaz.
  • Kod ile paylasim — programi tek satirlik sıkistirilmis koda cevir, karsi taraf yapistirip acar.
  • Checksum korumasi — kopyalamada bozulan kod aninda yakalanir, net uyari verilir.
  • Geriye donuk uyumluluk — eski formatlar (ANT1./ANT2./ANT4.) da acilir.
  • Veri cihazda — programlar ve ilerleme tarayici depolamasinda saklanir.

Nasil kullanilir?

  • Olustur: Gun gun kas grubu ve hareketleri gir, "Bitir & Olustur" de.
  • Takip et: Kutucuklari isaretle, kilolari gir, hafta sonunda "Tumunu Sifirla".
  • Paylas: Alttaki kodu "Kopyala" ile al. Instagram kodu bozabilir; WhatsApp, mail gibi uygulamalardan kodu paylasabilirsiniz.

What is Forge?

Create weekly workout programs, track them, and share them with a single code. Runs fully in the browser, no account needed.

Features

  • 7-day program wizard — add a muscle group and exercises for each day.
  • Workout tracking — check off completed exercises, log your weights.
  • Share via code — turn the program into a one-line compressed code; the recipient pastes and opens it.
  • Checksum protection — a corrupted copy is detected instantly with a clear message.
  • Backward compatible — legacy formats (ANT1./ANT2./ANT4.) still open.
  • Data stays on device — programs and progress are stored in browser storage.

How to use

  • Create: fill in muscle groups and exercises day by day, then hit "Finish & Create".
  • Track: tick the boxes, log weights, then "Reset All" at the end of the week.
  • Share: copy the code below with "Copy". Instagram can corrupt the code; you can share it via apps like WhatsApp or email.
